# ADR NNNN: <the decision, as a short phrase>

## Status: Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by ADR NNNN

## Context
What forced a choice? Two to five sentences: the situation, the constraint, what goes wrong without a decision. Plain facts, no solution yet.

## Options considered
1. Option A (one line)
2. Option B (one line)
3. Option C (one line, "do nothing" counts if it is a real option)

## Decision
The choice, in one or two sentences, in the present tense ("We use X").

## Why
The two or three reasons that decided it, tied to the context. Evidence beats opinion: a test you ran, a number you measured, a version you checked.

## Trade-offs accepted
What this costs or gives up. What would make us revisit it ("revisit if ...").
